AR Nav – pin test

Checks that pins stay where you put them. Open in Chrome on Android.

  1. Tap Start AR, allow the camera.
  2. Move the phone slowly over the floor until the circle turns green.
  3. Tap Drop pin with the circle on a tile corner.
  4. Walk 3 m away slowly, come back, and see whether the pin is still on the corner.
  5. Tap Exit, then Copy log, and paste it to Claude.
