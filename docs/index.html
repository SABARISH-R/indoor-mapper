AR Nav – pin test

Measures how far a pin drifts from its real spot. Open in Chrome on Android.

  1. Start AR. Hold the phone at chest height pointing ahead (like filming the room), and slowly pan left and right for 5 seconds.
  2. Look at the floor 1–2 m ahead until the circle turns green, put it on a tile corner, tap Drop.
  3. Walk to another room and back at normal pace, phone pointing ahead, not at your feet. If the screen says "Lift the phone", raise it.
  4. Put the circle on the real tile corner again and tap Check. It shows the drift in cm.
  5. Exit → Copy log → paste it to Claude.
